Loop

The cosy way to keep your place.

Loop is a warm, uncomplicated companion for crochet and knitting. Tap to count rows, organise every project, and catalogue your yarn stash — all offline, all in one calm little app.

What's inside

  • A giant tap-anywhere row counter that keeps the screen awake
  • Projects with counters, notes and a photo
  • Your whole yarn stash — brand, colourway, weight, fibre
  • A pattern library for PDFs, photos and links

Everything is stored on your device. No account, no sign-up, no network required.

Loop Plus

An optional subscription that removes the free limits: unlimited projects, counters, yarn and patterns. Monthly or yearly, cancel anytime in your App Store settings.
