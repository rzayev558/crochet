# Loop Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-07-22_

Loop is designed to work entirely on your device. We take the simplest possible
approach to privacy: we don't collect your data.

## What we collect
**Nothing.** Your projects, counters, yarn stash, patterns, photos and notes are
stored only in Loop's private storage on your device. They are never uploaded to
us and we have no servers that receive them.

## Photos and files
When you add a photo or import a pattern file, Loop copies it into the app's
private storage so it stays available offline. These files never leave your
device unless you choose to share them yourself.

## Purchases
If you subscribe to Loop Plus, the purchase is handled by Apple and by our
payments provider, RevenueCat, to verify your subscription status. Apple and
RevenueCat process purchase information under their own privacy policies:
- Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
- RevenueCat: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/

We receive only a non-identifying subscription status ("active" or not). We do
not receive your name, email or payment details.

## Children
Loop is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect any personal information from
anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and post the new version at
